Frozen pipeline per case (bench2/run_bench2.sh), serial, 180 min / 16 GiB each:
  hal-recover import -> groups (--mi-types counter,addition --mi-controls 3, 1 thread each)
  -> trace (--stimulus-json) -> analyze --protocol protocol.json --window <training length>
  -> cross-run taint check against a second trace with the same non-data pads and new data.
Measurements reported per case (no tuning between cases): certificate status, origin, period,
goals, rounds; data-carrying control nets per phase; groups and ordered groups; graph nodes,
excluded words, histories; registers split into >=2 write events; FIT_PROVED/raw counts;
periodic and whole-state replay; stage times. Algorithm-level readability (E3.3) and
C2 structural oracles (E2.1) are scored separately and later; their oracle construction
must be written down before scoring.
