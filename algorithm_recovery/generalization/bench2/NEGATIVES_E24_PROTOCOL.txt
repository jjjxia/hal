E2.4 negative cases, written 2026-11-02 BEFORE running them
===========================================================
Purpose: the system must either reject (no certificate / graph not closed / program refused)
or report the case as out of scope. A closed graph with a program that fails held-out or
fresh-input checks counts as a SILENT WRONG RESULT and is reported as such.

N1 data-dependent schedule: ibex_mul netlist and protocol unchanged except
   data_ind_timing_i = 0 (multiplier may finish early depending on op_b).
   Expected: protocol-relative certificate fails (FSM state depends on data), or the
   certified period does not match the 33-cycle transaction and program recovery is refused.
   Stages: prepare_bench2 -> trace -> certify -> (if certified) graph -> decompile.
N2 rotating binding and N3 hidden pipeline: constructed designs, to be specified before running.

N2 rotating binding (specified before running): constructed design rotbind.v, 3-cycle transaction,
   y = (a+b) + (a^b) on 16-bit operands; the two partial results are bound to registers r0/r1
   in even transactions and to r1/r0 in odd transactions (a data-independent toggle).
   Expected: certified schedule period = 2 transactions; program recovery refuses or the
   period multiple is reported; a closed one-transaction program would be a silent error.
