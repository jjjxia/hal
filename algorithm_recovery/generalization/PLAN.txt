Two independent-source generalization trials, frozen before execution (2026-11-01)
User instruction: choose two benchmarks and actually try them. Ordinary bounded
engineering/evaluation task; no autonomous research goal or benchmark expansion.
Recovery baseline: c57137ccbeb. Cases run serially, each at most 180 minutes and
16 GiB per-process virtual address space, MI/DANA one thread. No automatic push.

1. ZipCPU/dspfilters slowfil_srl
   Upstream 16d7dc3b23af167759693da9caff7c7111805873, rtl/slowfil_srl.v.
   LGPL-3.0-or-later RTL. Parameterized instance: LGNTAPS=3, NTAPS=7,
   IW=8, TW=8, OW=19, FIXED_TAPS=1. Seven signed taps 3,-5,7,11,-13,17,19;
   unused eighth coefficient zero. This is an explicitly configured upstream
   design, not the original 110-tap default or a new hand-built FIR.
   Coefficients become hardware ROM contents at synthesis, never recovery hints.
   Reset does not clear sample storage. Preserve a documented zero-sample flush
   prefix, then one sample every 16 clocks. Exact 7-tap signed integer sum is the
   evaluation reference; validate its alignment against upstream RTL first.
   Test: shared multiply/accumulate and delayed sample dependencies. A passing
   whole-state replay alone does not count as recognizing a FIR algorithm.

2. ZipCPU/cordic seqcordic
   Upstream 8ede08a1865d87a357a3a2c188206e843dd9bde6, rtl/seqcordic.v.
   LGPL-3.0-or-later RTL, unchanged checked-in widths IW/OW=13, WW=16, PW=20.
   Reset four rising edges, then start one transaction every 17 edges.
   Vary all octants, boundaries, signed inputs, auxiliary bit, then seeded random
   held-out tuples. Actual RTL outputs include rotations 1..14; preserve its
   NBA timing rather than substituting ideal trig or the pipelined architecture.
   Test: fixed iteration schedule with data-dependent sign/add/subtract guards,
   shared x/y/phase state, shift position and rounding. No new recognizer rule or
   hand-supplied CORDIC name/recurrence goes into recovery.
